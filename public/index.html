◉ Case Compass
Checking engine…

A case-prep companion that challenges you before the other side does.

Case Compass helps self-represented persons (SRPs) organise a dispute into facts, evidence and relevant law — then stress-tests it in role-play as the opposing party or a probing tribunal, so weak points surface in practice, not for the first time at a hearing.

1

Describe your dispute

Explain what happened in plain language. Targeted follow-up questions fill in the gaps.

2

See your Fact–Evidence–Law map

Everything you've shared is organised into what's supported, uncertain, or still missing.

3

Stress-test it

Face role-played objections (Opposing Party) or probing questions (Tribunal Questioning).

4

Get a readiness report

A neutral view of strengths, weak points, missing documents — never a prediction of outcome.

Responsible by design

  • No black-box conclusions. Legal points are linked to a named, checkable source — and flagged when unverified.
  • Confirmation-bias resistant. The system is built to surface counterarguments, not just validate your story.
  • Information, not representation. This is a preparation aid, not a lawyer, and never predicts a result.
  • Depends on you. Output quality depends entirely on the completeness and accuracy of what you provide.
